About

I’m an MSc student in Computer Science and Engineering at BUET, interested in exploring the intersection of Machine Learning and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). My research interests span Deep Learning, Large Language Models, and Human-Centered AI, with a focus on understanding intelligent systems from both computational and human perspectives.

I’m particularly interested in how deep learning models learn, reason, and perform, as well as how people interact with, perceive, and trust AI-driven technologies. I’m drawn to research that goes beyond model performance, bringing together technical innovation and human perspectives to create intelligent systems that are effective, accessible, and aligned with human needs.

Self-Tracking and Digital Health in Bangladesh

How do people in Bangladesh experience self-tracking tools — and what would make those tools feel safe, accessible, and supportive?

533survey respondents
49bilingual survey items
9in-depth interviews
Approach
  • Mixed methods: quantitative survey + Bangla–English interviews
  • Reliability, correlation, regression and Random Forest models, with subgroup fairness checks
  • Thematic coding plus embedding-based analysis of open-ended answers
Design implications
  • Privacy-by-default data handling
  • Bangla-first interaction
  • Compassionate, non-judgemental AI feedback
